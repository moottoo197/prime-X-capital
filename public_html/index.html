White Paper

Institutional Architecture, MSB Registration & Operational Governance

01 Platform Overview

PRIME X CAPITAL is an institutional-grade cryptocurrency trading, signal execution, and asset administration ecosystem built on zero-trust financial architecture. Every state transition is backed by atomic, double-entry ledger verification.

02 Market & Trading Architecture

PRIME X CAPITAL provides continuous liquidity aggregation across global exchanges. Support for Spot, Perpetual Futures with up to 100x leverage, Delivery Options, and quantitative institutional signal execution.

03 Double-Entry Accounting Ledger

Balances are strictly derived from ledger transaction logs. Every deposit, withdrawal, trade margin lock, and internal transfer records equal debits and credits with balance verification before and after.

04 Four-Eyes Governance & Security

High-value transactions and withdrawals exceeding 1,000 USDT require dual authorization from two distinct administrative actors. All administrative mutations produce tamper-evident audit logs.

05 KYC & Regulatory Compliance

Encrypted document storage with private object storage buckets. Identity documents are validated for MIME integrity, file signatures, and protected by role-based compliance review desks.

06 Strategic Roadmap

Phase 1: Zero-trust core ledger and mobile trading client. Phase 2: Automated signal execution and real-time orderbook settlement. Phase 3: Institutional multi-region replication and multi-sig cold custody.
